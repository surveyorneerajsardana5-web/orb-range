ENTRY STATUS RULES · COLOUR GUIDE

Click to view rules ＋
🔵 WAIT · BLUE BLINKINGPrice is inside 09:15–09:45 Opening Range, or no completed 5-minute candle has closed outside it. CALL/PUT entry is blocked.
🟣 BREAKOUT CONFIRMED · PURPLE BLINKINGA completed 5-minute candle closes above Opening Range High (B0). This is breakout confirmation only—not an entry. Wait for B1.
🩷 BREAKDOWN CONFIRMED · PINK BLINKINGA completed 5-minute candle closes below Opening Range Low (B0). This is breakdown confirmation only—not an entry. Wait for B1.
🟢 B1 BREAKOUT ENTRY CONFIRMED · GREEN BLINKINGAfter upside B0, the next completed 5-minute B1 candle retests and holds the range high, or continues and closes above it. Only then show CALL entry-ready; verify risk and filters.
🔴 B1 BREAKDOWN ENTRY CONFIRMED · RED BLINKINGAfter downside B0, the next completed 5-minute B1 candle retests and holds below the range low, or continues and closes below it. Only then show PUT entry-ready; verify risk and filters.
🟡 INVALIDATED · NO ENTRYIf price returns inside the range or B1 fails to hold/continue beyond the range edge, cancel the setup and wait for a fresh B0 break plus B1 confirmation.

Range: 09:15–09:45 IST · Signals use completed 5-minute candles · No entry on wick-only breaks · Paper trading first; confirmation does not guarantee profit.
